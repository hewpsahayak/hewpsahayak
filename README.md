# HEWP Sahayak Tool

**HEWP Sahayak Tool** is an independent automation toolkit — by **MRGARGSIR** — for engineers and contractors working with the Haryana Engineering Works Portal (HEWP). It cuts BOQ preparation, billing, and variation work down from hours to minutes.

> This is an independent third-party tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the HEWP portal or any government department.

## What it does

- **BOQ Preparation** — build Bill of Quantities directly in Excel, formatted for HEWP upload
- **Billing & Variation** — prepare running/final bills and variation statements automatically
- **Metal Weight Calculator** — quick steel/metal weight estimation for civil works
- **Material Consumption** — auto-generate consumption and abstract statements
- **Auto Upload / Auto Convert** — push prepared data straight to the HEWP portal, and convert between PDF ⇄ Excel
- **HSR Search** — look up Haryana Schedule of Rates items inside Excel
